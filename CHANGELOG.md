# Changelog

## v1.0.0-beta.1

### Added

- Windows-compatible Dashboard, API and public read-only access.
- Full historical market-data foundation with incremental update architecture and Feature cache.
- Candidate Signals, Trade Plans and deterministic system Paper Trading lifecycle.
- Paper Account, Orders, Fills, Positions, Entry/Exit Engine, Equity Curve and Strategy Scoreboard.
- Versioned Trade Review with immutable context snapshots and objective metrics.
- Market Snapshot, Symbol Overview and Gemini-backed AI Companion.
- Single production Telegram Bot with persistent Chinese/English language flow.
- Feedback workflow, administrator notifications, Preview parity and official brand assets.

### Safety

- Paper Trading Only; no real broker orders or real position synchronization.
- Reserved Telegram Bots remain disabled.
- Credentials, databases, backups and runtime logs remain outside Git.

### Known limitations

- Strategy samples remain limited and Trade Plan count may be zero.
- Reliable Sharpe is not available.
- Telegram avatar setup is manual through BotFather.
- Long-term stability and full restore rehearsal remain Beta validation work.
